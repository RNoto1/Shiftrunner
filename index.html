💡 Store Operating Funds

Understand the money pool

What Is Store Operating Funds?

Think of it as one big pool of money (e.g., $3,000) split into two buckets:

  • Change Fund — Money kept in the safe.
  • Tills — Money sitting in registers.
Money moves between these buckets, but the total pool never changes. By end of day, you must make it "whole."
